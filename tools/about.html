About nextchapter

nextchapter brings together Larry Reynolds’s professional context and personal career tools: résumé tailoring, portfolio positioning, interview preparation, and opportunity evaluation.

The tools

Career Radar

A future home for job discovery and opportunity evaluation. Career Radar is currently a placeholder; discovery and evaluation are not yet available.

Prospect Tracker

Manage prospects and applications with a status board, editable details, and status history. Preview job postings from Greenhouse, Ashby, Lever, and supported Workday sites, then review and save their information.

Résumé Builder

Create tailored résumés and cover letters, review and revise them, and download an application package with PDF and Word documents and an ATS-plain résumé.

How the tools work together

Track an opportunity in Prospect Tracker, then use “Send to Résumé Builder” to carry its job information into tailored application materials. Career Radar will add discovery and evaluation in a future phase.

Shared context, separate responsibilities

The tools share canonical career facts, supporting experience, and writing guidance. Opportunity records remain separate from that context: tracking a company does not mean publishing it.

The project’s README and source repository explain the tools, their shared context, and how they are maintained.
